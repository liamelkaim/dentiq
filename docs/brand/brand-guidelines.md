# Dentiq brand guidelines (v1 concept)

**Name:** Dentiq (capital D, lowercase entiq). **Slogan:** LEARN · SHARE · EXCEL.

**Positioning:** A student-centered, premium digital learning platform for dentistry students. Not a clinic-management system and not affiliated with Université de Montréal.

## Identity
- Primary wordmark: `logos/dentiq-horizontal-dark.svg` or `...light.svg` depending on surface.
- Secondary lockup with slogan: `logos/dentiq-stacked-*.svg`.
- Small-screen and favicon mark: `icons/dentiq-app-*.svg`, with PNG equivalents.
- The logo uses a geometric tooth outline to stay recognizable at small sizes. The mark is purposefully simple, rather than a glossy 3-D effect.
- Never distort, rotate, recolor inconsistently, or add glow effects to functional navigation assets.
- Minimum suggested logo height: 24 CSS px; icon: 20 CSS px.
- Clear space: at least half the tooth icon width around the mark.

## Typography
Primary: Inter (self-host or suitable font provider subject to license). Fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif. No font files distributed.

## UI art direction
- Default appearance: cinematic, dark, calm, intentional and highly readable.
- Mountain imagery allowed sparingly as a subdued dashboard banner, **not** behind reading content.
- Study Mode: dense, legible focused notes workspace with sidebar, tabs (Notes / Passes / Flashcards / Recordings / AI Tutor), and a distraction-free reading panel.
- Light theme must feel designed, not inverted.
- Desktop sidebar, tablet compact navigation, mobile bottom navigation when appropriate.
- Avoid gratuitous glassmorphism, gradients, over-rounded cards and generic AI-dashboard styling.

## Legal / brand readiness
Brand identity is a concept. No trademark search or domain clearance has been performed. Review prior to a public launch. Do not claim university endorsement.
